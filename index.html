A few practical questions.

Can you take over an existing AWS environment?

Yes. We start by reviewing the account, workloads, permissions and available documentation, then agree on an onboarding plan and the ongoing responsibilities.

Do we keep ownership of our accounts?

Yes. Your company retains ownership of its cloud accounts, domains and data. Administrative access is granted as needed for the agreed work.

What does the service cost?

Pricing depends on your users, servers, applications, existing setup and support needs. Ongoing administration is quoted as a monthly service; projects are estimated separately. Cloud usage, software licences and other third-party costs are identified separately.

Is support remote or on-site?

Services are primarily delivered remotely. For Edmonton-area businesses, any required on-site work can be discussed and included in the proposal. Support hours and response expectations are agreed before service begins.

Can you help with just one part of our IT?

Yes. You can engage Netralis for AWS operations, a server, Microsoft 365 administration or a specific project. We can coordinate with your existing software vendors or IT provider.
